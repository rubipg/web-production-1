Tunnels All Around

As you make your way deeper beneath the Bikini Bottom, you come to a halt, confronted by three different paths. The first tunnel is narrow and winding with faint eerie sounds echohing from its depths, it might be risky but could it lead to the secret Krusty Krab formula? The second path is wider and well lit as if someone or something has traveled it recently. Is it a trap or does it lead directly to the prize? The third path is dark and uninviting with no clear signs of where it might go. It's the ultimate gamble, but what if its the quickest route to victory?

Which path will you choose?
