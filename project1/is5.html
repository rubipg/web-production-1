Fancy Food Critic

As the disguised food critic, you step into the Krusty Krab with an air of authority, clipboard in hand, and a notepad ready. The smell of the famous Krabby Patty drifted towards you. You know this is your chance to get closer to the secret formula, but the next move is critical.

Do you play it safe by inspecting the Krabby Patty closely to note its ingredients and structure? You could analyze the layers, the texture of the patty, and the precise composition of the sauce, every detail could be a clue.

Or do you go bold and take a quick bite? A single taste might reveal what no amount of looking ever could, the blend of spices, the cooking technique, the balance of flavours. But you'll need to act fast to avoid suspicion.

Every second counts and your choice here could bring you one step closer to ultimate success or disaster. What will it be?
