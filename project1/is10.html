YOU LOSE

You couldn't resist. Dressed in your clever disguise as a food critic, you had the Krabby Patty in your tiny hands, ready to analyze its every detail. But then, temptation got the best of you. Instead of sticking to your plan, you took a quick bite. The flavor was overwhelming and so delicious that you tried not to chew hoping no one noticed your facial expression, but Mr. Krab did. His sharp eyes narrowed as he leaned closer, his suspicion growing with every passing second. "That ain't no ordinary critic" he muttered. Before you could make an excuse, he grabbed your disguse, yanking it away to reveal your true identity. He made you vomit the patty and sent you back to the Chum bucket in a cannon!
