Pickle Costume Fail

You decided to disguise yourself as a pickle, thinking it would be the perfect way to sneak past Mr. Krab and his customers. But you took it too far that your disguise was so convincing that a hungry customer almost took a bit out of you! Panic sets in as you realize you're moments away from being eaten alive, and the thought of your tiny, green body in someones's stomach is too mush to bear. Desperation hits, you can't die like this. You can't fail now. In a split second, you abandon your plan, fleeing back to the Chum Bucket in defeat, heart racing. Its not how you pictured it, but survivial comes first. You'll regroup, rethink, and try again.
